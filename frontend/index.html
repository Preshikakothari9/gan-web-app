How GAN Training Works
Step 1 — Sample Noise
Random vector z is sampled from a normal distribution and fed into the Generator G.
Step 2 — Train Discriminator
D sees real samples (label=1) and G's fake output (label=0). Binary Cross-Entropy loss is backpropagated through D.
Step 3 — Train Generator
G's fake output is fed to D. G is updated to maximise D's output (fool D into saying fake=real). Loss flows back through D into G.
